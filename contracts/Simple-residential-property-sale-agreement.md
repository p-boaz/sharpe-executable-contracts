# RESIDENTIAL PROPERTY SALE AGREEMENT

This Property Sale Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on April 1, 2026, between Alice Sellers ("Seller") and Bob Buyers ("Buyer").

## 1. Property

Seller agrees to sell, and Buyer agrees to purchase, the residential property located at 42 Maple Lane, Springfield (the "Property").

## 2. Purchase Price

The total purchase price for the Property is $500,000 (the "Purchase Price").

## 3. Earnest Money Deposit

Buyer shall pay an earnest money deposit of $25,000 to Seller no later than April 8, 2026. The deposit shall be credited toward the Purchase Price at closing.

## 4. Closing

Closing shall occur on or before June 1, 2026 (the "Closing Date"). At closing, Buyer shall pay the remaining balance of $475,000, and Seller shall deliver a signed deed transferring title to Buyer.

## 5. Late Payment Fee

If Buyer fails to pay the remaining balance by the Closing Date, Buyer shall owe Seller a late fee of $1,000 for each day the payment is overdue, up to a maximum of $30,000.

## 6. Default

If Buyer fails to pay the earnest money deposit by April 8, 2026, Seller may terminate this Agreement and retain any amounts already paid.

## 7. Condition of Property

Seller shall deliver the Property in its current condition, with all fixtures and appliances included.

## 8. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.
